Upload to Shopify

In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes → Import theme → Upload zip file. Select 75th-Avenue-Shopify-Theme.zip as a draft theme.

Shopify’s upload instructions →

Switch from demos to your products

  1. Add the actual coffee products to Shopify with your confirmed names, photos, descriptions, inventory, and price of 25.99 in the intended store currency.
  2. Put those products in a collection and select it in the Home page’s Collection V2 section.
  3. Turn off “Show sample products when empty” in Collection V2 and “Show sample collection when empty” in the collection template when you are ready for the real catalog.
  4. The Shop page automatically renders actual products when its Shopify collection contains products. Native Shopify filters, sorting, pagination, product forms, and cart replace the demonstration flow.
  5. Configure available storefront filters in Shopify’s Search & Discovery settings; the theme renders the filters Shopify supplies.

Demo items cannot be purchased and are not imported as Shopify product records. Their displayed $25.99 price is your planned 12 oz price, not a price copied from Glozin. The packaging and names belong to the demo catalog.

Customize the design

  • Change global brand colors and upload a replacement logo through Brand settings.
  • Edit the hero heading, description, and photograph in Hero V2.
  • Reorder or remove Home sections using the theme editor. Edit Founder Story text, Southwest Roots region blocks, and the Coffee Passion heading and description there too.
  • Replace the demonstration photos in Promos V2 and Story V2 in their Liquid section files, or add image-picker settings as the photography is finalized.
  • The header deliberately keeps the focus on Home and Shop, with an on-page story link and the quiz popup.
  • Configure Shopify’s native newsletter, policy links, payment methods, shipping, and taxes for launch.
